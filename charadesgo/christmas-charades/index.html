Play with the Christmas Classics deck

Charades GO is the FREE family party game, and its new Christmas Classics deck is free to play like every other deck. It’s made of Christmas movies, songs and storybook favorites.

  1. Open Charades GO and pick the Christmas Classics deck.
  2. Hold the phone to your forehead so everyone but you can see the screen.
  3. Everyone else shouts clues, acts it out or hums it.
  4. Tilt down when you guess it. Tilt up to pass.
  5. Pass the phone to the next guesser.

Tips for Christmas charades

  • Theme each round: movies first, then songs, then characters.
  • Let kids hum the songs. Humming makes song titles possible for little ones.
  • Make it a family tradition. Play the same night every year and keep a running scoreboard.
