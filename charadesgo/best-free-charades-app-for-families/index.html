How a family round works

  1. Pick a deck everyone can enjoy.
  2. The guesser holds the phone to their forehead so everyone but them can see the screen.
  3. The family shouts clues, acts it out or hums it, all at once.
  4. Tilt down when the guesser gets it. Tilt up to pass.
  5. Hand the phone to the next guesser and go again.

Tips for a great family game night

  • Start with an easy deck. Quick wins warm up shy players.
  • Pair little kids with a grown-up who helps them act and shout clues.
  • Mix ages on each team so older kids can help younger ones.
  • Theme the night. Try the holiday decks in season, or pick animals for a silly round.
  • Let the clue-givers get loud. In Charades GO, clues can be shouted, acted or hummed.
