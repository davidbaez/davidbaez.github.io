How to play charades on iPhone

Charades GO: The FREE family party game

To play charades on iPhone, open Charades GO, pick a free deck, hold the phone to your forehead with the screen facing out, and tilt down when you guess it or up to pass.

The phone version is a faster, louder take on classic charades: nobody has to write slips of paper, and the person guessing can’t see the screen. For the silent, traditional rules and hand signals, read how to play charades.

What you need

  • An iPhone with Charades GO installed (it’s free)
  • At least 2 players, and up to 100+ if they fit in the room
  • No internet, no slips of paper and no account

How to play, step by step

  1. Pick a deck. Charades GO has 60+ free themed decks.
  2. Hold the phone to your forehead with the screen facing out, so everyone but you can see the word.
  3. Everyone else gives clues all at once. Shout, act it out, hum it or do an impression.
  4. Tilt down when you guess it. Tilt up to pass.
  5. Hand the phone to the next guesser and go again.

Which deck should you pick?

  • Act It Out for a very physical round.
  • Hum That Tune when you want to hum instead of talk.
  • Animalistic for animal noises and silly movements.
  • Made for Kids or Emoji when young players are guessing. Emoji uses picture cards for kids who can’t read yet.
  • Everything when you want a mix of many decks for variety.

Tips for a better round

  • Start easy. A quick first deck gets everyone laughing.
  • Pair little kids with a grown-up to help them with clues.
  • Keep score your own way. Count the correct guesses per person, or split into teams and take turns.
  • Make a house rule or two. For example, no saying any part of the word.
  • Mix the decks. Switch decks between rounds to keep the game fresh.

Fun ways to change it up

  • Silent round: clue-givers act only, no talking.
  • Sound effects only: no gestures, just noises. Great with Animalistic.
  • Hum only: try it with Hum That Tune.

No. Charades GO works offline.

How many people can play charades on one iPhone?

From 2 players to 100+ (if they fit in the room). You pass one phone to the next guesser.

Do I need to write words on slips of paper?

No. The phone shows the words, so there are no slips to write, fold or argue over.

Can the clue-givers talk?

Yes. In the phone version, everyone else can shout clues, act it out or hum it, all at once.
