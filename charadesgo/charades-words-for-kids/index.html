Charades words for kids

Easy, act-it-out ideas sorted by age and theme.

The best charades words for kids are things they can see and do: animals, actions, foods and people they meet every day. Pick a list that fits your youngest player, read the word into their ear if they can’t read yet, and let them go.

New to the game? Start with how to play charades.

Tips for playing charades with kids

  • Let them talk. For kids under 7, allow sound effects. Silent acting is hard when you’re five.
  • Use longer rounds. Give little ones 90 seconds or more instead of the usual 60.
  • Celebrate every guess. Skip strict scoring and cheer the actor too.
  • Mix ages on each team so older kids help younger ones.
