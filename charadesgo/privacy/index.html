Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts. Charades GO never asks for your name, email address or phone number.
  • We use analytics and crash reporting to keep the game running well, and ads to keep every deck free.
  • We check whether you found the app through one of our ads, so we know which ads are worth paying for.
  • Subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Who we are

Charades GO is an iPhone and iPad game made by David Baez (“we” or “us”). This policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, who collects it, and the choices you have.

What we don’t collect

You don’t create an account to play, and we don’t ask you for personal details such as your name, email address, phone number or contacts.

What is collected, and by whom

The app uses a few third-party services. Each one collects information automatically, mostly technical details about your device and how the app is used.

Analytics: Google Analytics for Firebase

Firebase tells us how the game is used: things like which decks are played, how long rounds last and which screens are visited. It also records basic device information (device model, iOS version, approximate country) and an app instance identifier, a random ID generated for your install of the app. When you install the app after seeing one of our Google ads, Firebase also helps Google count that install toward the ad campaign.

Performance and crash reporting: New Relic

New Relic helps us find and fix crashes, slowdowns and errors. It receives technical details such as device model, iOS version, app version and what the app was doing when a problem occurred. We also use it to record how you found the app (see “Ad attribution” below).

Advertising: Google AdMob

The free version of Charades GO shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may use device identifiers (including Apple’s advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), your IP address and general device information. Whether the ads you see are personalized depends on your iOS tracking settings (see “Your choices” below). Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Ad attribution: Apple Ads

When you first open the app, it asks Apple whether the download came from one of our Apple Ads campaigns. Apple answers with information about the campaign (for example, a campaign or keyword number), not information about you. We save that answer on your device in the iOS Keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, it may sync to your other Apple devices, and it may remain in your Keychain after you delete the app. We also send the campaign details to New Relic so we can see which ads bring in players who enjoy the game.

Subscriptions: Apple

Charades GO+ is purchased and managed through your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment. We only learn whether a subscription is active, never your name or payment information.

How we use this information

  • To run the game and fix bugs and crashes
  • To understand which features and decks people enjoy, so we can make better ones
  • To show ads that keep all of our decks free
  • To learn which of our ads actually work, so we spend our marketing budget wisely

We don’t sell lists of users or personal details to data brokers, and we don’t use this information to identify you personally.

Children

Charades GO is a game for players of all ages, and kids often play it with their families. We don’t ask anyone, including children, to provide personal information such as a name, email address or photo. The technical information described above is collected the same way for every player. If you’re a parent or guardian and believe information about your child has been collected in a way you don’t want, email us and we’ll help.

Your choices

  • Ad tracking: In iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, you can stop apps from asking to track you, or turn tracking off for Charades GO.
  • Apple’s own ads: In Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, you can turn off personalized ads from Apple.
  • Remove ads: Charades GO+ subscribers don’t see ads.
  • Stop collection: Deleting the app stops all collection from your device.

Keeping and protecting information

Information collected by Firebase, New Relic and AdMob is stored and protected by those services and kept according to their own retention settings and policies. We keep it only as long as it’s useful for the purposes above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to have it deleted, or to object to how it’s used. Because we don’t collect names or emails, we usually can’t match data to a specific person, but email us and we’ll do everything we reasonably can.
