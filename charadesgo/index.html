The free charades app that’s actually free.

Charades GO is the word guessing party game everyone needs on their iPhone. Every single deck is 100% free to play, forever. All 58+ of them. No paywalls, no locked categories, no forced subscriptions.

We add 1–2 new decks every week and don’t plan on stopping, so there’s always something fresh for your next game night.

How to play

  1. Pick one of 58+ free themed decks.
  2. Hold the phone to your forehead so everyone but you can see the screen.
  3. Your friends and family shout clues, act it out, or hum it, all at once, in glorious chaos.
  4. Tilt down when you guess it. Tilt up to pass.
  5. Hand the phone to the next guesser and go again!

Why people pick Charades GO

  • Every deck is free. Most charades apps give you a handful of decks and lock the rest.
  • Works offline. Play on a plane, at the cabin, anywhere.
  • Any group size. From 2 players to 100+ (if they fit in the room).
  • All ages. Rated 4+, with decks made for little kids.
