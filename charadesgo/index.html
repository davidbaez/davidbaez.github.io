Charades GO

The free charades app that’s actually free.

Charades GO is the word guessing party game everyone needs on their iPhone. Every single category in our game is 100% free to play, forever. All 57+ of them. No paywalls or forced subscriptions.

We add 1–2 categories to our library every single week, and we do not plan on ever stopping. So you can count on always having fresh categories ready for your next party.

How to play

  1. Select from our large list of 57+ free themed categories.
  2. Hold the phone to your forehead so everyone but you can see the screen.
  3. Your friends and family will yell out clues for the word on the screen, all at the same time in a very chaotic manner!
  4. Tilt down if you guessed correctly and tilt up to pass the current word.
  5. Pass the phone to the next guesser and repeat!

Categories include

  • Everything Deck (a combination of many categories for variety)
  • Act It Out
  • Animalistic
  • Fast Food
  • Made For Kids (easy deck for young readers)
  • Sound It Out
  • Accents
  • Brands
  • Disney
  • Everyday Things
  • And many more!

Charades GO is a local multiplayer game that can be played with as few as 2 people, or as many as 100+ people (if they fit in the room). It is suitable for all ages and playable fully offline. It is the perfect addition for any game night or family gathering. We hope you enjoy our free charades game!
