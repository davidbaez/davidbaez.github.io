Tips for a Thanksgiving charades night

  • Play right after dinner, while everyone’s still at the table and before people scatter.
  • Make teams by generation. Kids vs. parents vs. grandparents is always a crowd-pleaser.
  • Pass the phone instead of writing slips. Nobody has to hunt for pens, and you can play the second the plates are cleared.
